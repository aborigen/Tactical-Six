# Tactical Six | AI Engine Operational Intelligence

The **Tactical Six** engine is a deterministic, local-first tactical analyzer designed for high-intensity 6x6 strategic engagement. It operates entirely within the browser's execution environment using a sophisticated search-and-evaluate architecture.

## 🧠 Core Architecture: Minimax Algorithm

At its heart, the engine uses the **Minimax** algorithm. This is a recursive decision-making process that simulates the "perfect" game.

1.  **Search Tree**: The AI generates a tree of every possible legal move.
2.  **Lookahead**: For every move the AI considers (Maximizing), it assumes the opponent will respond with their best possible counter-move (Minimizing).
3.  **Recursive Evaluation**: The engine repeats this process down to the selected **Search Depth** (e.g., Depth 5 for Grandmaster difficulty).

## ⚡ Optimization: Alpha-Beta Pruning

Evaluating every possible move in chess leads to an exponential explosion of data. Tactical Six uses **Alpha-Beta Pruning** to stay performant:

-   **Alpha**: The best score the maximizing player (AI) is guaranteed.
-   **Beta**: The best score the minimizing player (Opponent) is guaranteed.
-   **Pruning**: If the engine finds a branch that is definitively worse than a move it has already evaluated, it stops searching that branch immediately. This allows the engine to search much deeper in the same amount of time.

## 🎯 Evaluation Logic: Beyond Material Counting

The engine doesn't just count pieces; it understands the geometry of the 6x6 battlefield.

### 1. Material Weighting
Each piece is assigned a tactical value:
-   **Pawn**: 100
-   **Knight**: 320
-   **Bishop**: 330
-   **Rook**: 500
-   **Queen**: 900
-   **King**: 20,000 (Infinite priority)

### 2. Piece-Square Tables (PST)
The engine uses **Positional Heuristics**. Every square on the 6x6 matrix has a hidden value for each piece type:
-   **Pawns** gain value as they approach the promotion rank.
-   **Knights** lose value at the edges where their mobility is restricted (the "Knight on the rim is dim" principle).
-   **Bishops** and **Queens** are encouraged to control the central diagonals.
-   **Kings** are incentivized to stay in protected corners during the opening and mid-game.

## 🚀 Efficiency: Move Ordering (MVV-LVA)

To make Alpha-Beta pruning even more effective, the engine sorts moves before searching them using the **Most Valuable Victim - Least Valuable Attacker** heuristic.

By evaluating captures (e.g., a Pawn taking a Queen) and promotions first, the engine hits "Beta cut-offs" faster, significantly reducing the number of nodes it needs to visit.

## 🕹️ Difficulty Matrix

The search depth directly correlates to the engine's "foresight":
-   **Recruit (Depth 1)**: Only sees immediate captures.
-   **Cadet (Depth 2)**: Sees one full turn ahead (Basic traps).
-   **Specialist (Depth 3)**: Balanced positional play.
-   **Commander (Depth 4)**: Advanced tactical coordination.
-   **Grandmaster (Depth 5)**: Deep-state analysis capable of identifying complex multi-move combinations.
